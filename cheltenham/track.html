Cheltenham Festival Stats

The Track

Four days, 27 races and two courses – the Old Course and the New Course.

Old Course– Day One and Two – Champion Hurdle and Queen Mother Champion Chase

New Course – Day Three and Four – World Hurdle and Cheltenham Gold Cup

The Old Course – both Cheltenham courses are galloping, left handed tracks presenting some of the most difficult jumping tests in the land. Whether on the Old or New, the unique undulations of Prestbury Park make it a demanding test and ensure course form is a priceless currency.

The New Course– The Festival moves to the New Course for days three and four – providing fresh ground after an intense two days of racing. On the hurdles track, fast finishers tend to go well on the New Course where there are only two hurdles to clear in the last three-quarters of a mile.

Cheltenham Facts:

  • During the Festival, 200 tonnes of divot mix will be used in treading / repairing the course and 100 sacks of grass seed.
  • There are 10 miles of running rail on the racecourse with 165 hurdles and 24 fences - measuring a quarter-mile long if they were all joined together.
  • After the Festival, there are around 4 million hoofprints on the racecourse!
